Before you ask

What does a typical engagement look like?

Most start as a 3-week scoped build — a defined slice with a clear deliverable. From there it usually becomes an ongoing retainer or a longer contract. I prefer fixed-scope phases over hourly drift.

What's your rate?

Depends on scope, region, and whether you need senior architecture or implementation hands. You get a flat phase quote, not an hourly bill, after a 30-minute scoping call.

Where are your clients based?

UAE, Saudi Arabia, the United States, and Egypt. I run on Gulf time but overlap comfortably with GCC, EU, and US East / Central business hours.

Can you take a project from zero?

Yes — that's where I'm strongest. Architecture, schema, deploy, the first slice in production. I can stay through scale or hand it off cleanly.
